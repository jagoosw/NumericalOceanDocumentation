NumericalOcean1-JRA55_v0.1.0

As built by forced_ocean_simulation(ORCA1(GPU())) with default arguments.

GrideORCA1 tripolar ($1\degree$), 70 z-star levels to 5500 m (1.5 m at the surface)
Time step90 minutes, split Runge–Kutta 3 (Silvestri et al., 2026), split-explicit free surface
Equation of stateTEOS-10 (Conservative Temperature and Absolute Salinity)
Momentum advectionWENO vector invariant, 5th order (Silvestri et al., 2024)
Tracer advectionWENO, 7th order
Vertical mixingCATKE (Wagner et al., 2025)
Background diffusivityκ = max(2×10⁻⁶, 10⁻⁵ |sin φ|) m² s⁻¹ (Henyey et al., 1986), ν = 3×10⁻⁵ m² s⁻¹
Mesoscale eddiesGM (Gent & McWilliams, 1990) and Redi (Redi, 1982) on triads (Griffies et al., 1998), κ = 800 m² s⁻¹
Horizontal viscositybiharmonic, ν₄ = (cell area)² / 50 days
Sea iceClimaSeaIce, 4 thickness categories, EVP dynamics (Kimmritz et al., 2017), landfast basal stress (Lemieux et al., 2015), incremental remapping (Lipscomb & Hunke, 2004)
Atmosphere and runoffJRA55-do 1958–2018 (Tsujino et al., 2018)
Air-sea fluxesCOARE similarity theory (Edson et al., 2013)
Ice-ocean heat fluxthree-equation (Holland & Jenkins, 1999)
Shortwave penetrationtwo-band, SeaWiFS chlorophyll climatology
Salinity restoringto monthly WOA, piston velocity 0.254 m day⁻¹
Initial conditionsWOA annual temperature and salinity, ECCO4 sea ice (January 1993), ocean at rest
Spin-up5 cycles of 1958–2018 JRA55
